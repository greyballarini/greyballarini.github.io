This code simultaneously turns on three sets of LED lights with three different blink patterns to simulate the behavior of fireflies.

![output](https://greyballarini.github.io/assets/img/Simultaneous_Sparklies.jpg)

A tip I would give to someone attempting this project would be to not second guess how easy the three codes really are to put together. I was doubting if it was really correct to just copy and paste when I should have trusted the process.
