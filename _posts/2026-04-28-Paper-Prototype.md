A paper prototype of my final project: a light up screaming opossum plush. This prototype is mostly to map the circuits.

![output](https://greyballarini.github.io/assets/img/paper_prototype.jpg)

I made a change to my design, which is that the eyes no longer light up (because I think it would be a shame to mess with the plush face). Because his eyes don't shine like a typical nocturnal critter, he will have pink lights in his ears as an aid.

![output](https://greyballarini.github.io/assets/img/opossum_face.jpg)
