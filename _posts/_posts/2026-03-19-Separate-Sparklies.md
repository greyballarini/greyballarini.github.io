These three codes activate three LED lights in three different patterns. These simulate three varying firefly flashing patterns.

![flashbulb](https://greyballarini.github.io/assets/img/flashbulb.jpg)
FLASH BULB: flashes for a half second ever four seconds.

![bigdipper](https://greyballarini.github.io/assets/img/bigdipper.jpg)
BIG DIPPER: fades linearly to max brightness every three seconds.

![synchronous](https://greyballarini.github.io/assets/img/synchronous.jpg)
SYNCHRONOUS: flashes in a series of three every ten seconds.

A tip I would give to someone attempting this project would be to work as thoroughly as possible on the worksheets in order to make the coding process easier.
